Verification changes since November 8

The November 9 declared figures contain revisions in 17 ridings compared with the November 8, 4:21 p.m. dataset. Net changes: NDP +13 votes; Conservative +8; Green unchanged; Other −12; rejected ballots +4. Total ballots considered rose by 13. No riding's leading party changed.

Two automatic judicial recounts pending

Elections BC lists Kelowna Centre (Conservatives +43) and Ladysmith-Oceanside (BC NDP +50). Applications are due November 16. The BC Supreme Court sets the recount dates.

The Conservatives were declared elected by 64 votes (6,570 to 6,506) after taking the lead during final counting. Elections BC scheduled a district electoral officer recount before final count. Elections BC lists two ridings for automatic judicial recounts, shown below.

Methodology and sources

2024 baseline: Elections BC's certified Statement of Votes. 2026: Elections BC's November 9 declared Party Report. Party shares use total valid votes. Rejected ballots are excluded from those shares. Preliminary provincial participation uses total ballots considered divided by 3,738,182 registered voters recorded October 14, 2026; the registration reference dates differ from 2024. Riding-level ballot-count changes must not be presented as turnout-rate changes.
